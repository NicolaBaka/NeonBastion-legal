Politique de confidentialité
Neon Bastion: Tower Defense

Version 1.1 — en vigueur au 1er octobre 2026 (version 1.0 du 7 juillet 2026)

En bref : Neon Bastion ne demande aucun compte : votre progression, votre pseudo et votre portrait restent sur votre appareil. Le Jeu intègre des services tiers : la publicité (Google AdMob), les achats intégrés (Google Play / App Store), la sauvegarde cloud optionnelle (Google Play Jeux / Apple Game Center), ainsi que des statistiques d'usage et des rapports de plantage (Google Firebase) qui nous servent à améliorer le Jeu. Les statistiques ne sont activées que si vous acceptez la mesure d'audience dans le formulaire de consentement, et tout se coupe à tout moment dans les réglages. Aucune donnée n'est vendue. Aucun profil nominatif n'est constitué par l'Éditeur.

2. Champ d'application

Cette politique couvre les traitements de données réalisés au sein du Jeu sur Android et iOS, y compris ceux opérés par les SDK tiers que le Jeu intègre. Elle ne couvre pas les traitements réalisés par Google Play, l'App Store, ou votre fabricant d'appareil en dehors du Jeu, qui relèvent de leurs politiques respectives.

3.8 Notifications

Les rappels du Jeu (récompense disponible, recherche terminée…) sont des notifications locales, programmées sur votre appareil : aucun serveur ni jeton de notification n'est utilisé. L'autorisation est demandée après votre première partie, et vous pouvez la retirer à tout moment dans les réglages de votre appareil.

3.9 Vérification de l'heure

Pour protéger les minuteries du Jeu (recherches, récompenses quotidiennes) contre le changement d'heure de l'appareil, le Jeu lit l'heure officielle via une requête technique vers un serveur de Google (www.google.com), au lancement et au retour dans l'app. Aucune donnée de jeu ni identifiant n'est envoyé : seule votre adresse IP est, comme pour toute connexion, visible de Google.

3.10 Ce que nous n'utilisons pas

Le Jeu n'intègre aucun réseau social ni aucun autre outil d'analyse que Firebase (pas de Facebook, AppsFlyer, etc.), et aucun suivi publicitaire entre applications autre que celui lié à la publicité décrite au §3.4. Les statistiques du §3.6 ne sont ni vendues, ni partagées à des fins publicitaires.

4. Destinataires et transferts hors UE

Les seules entités recevant des données sont Google (AdMob, Play Jeux, Play Billing, Firebase) et Apple (Game Center, StoreKit), chacune pour les traitements décrits ci-dessus. Ces fournisseurs peuvent transférer des données vers des pays tiers (notamment les États-Unis) ; ces transferts sont encadrés par le EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework et/ou des clauses contractuelles types (art. 46 RGPD).

Privacy Policy
Neon Bastion: Tower Defense

Version 1.1 — effective October 1, 2026 (version 1.0 of July 7, 2026)

In short: Neon Bastion requires no account: your progress, nickname and portrait stay on your device. The game integrates third-party services: advertising (Google AdMob), in-app purchases (Google Play / App Store), optional cloud saves (Google Play Games / Apple Game Center), and usage statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase), which help us improve the game. Statistics are only enabled if you accept audience measurement in the consent form, and everything can be turned off at any time in the settings. No data is ever sold.

1. Data controller

Neon Bastion: Tower Defense (com.getkapi.neonbastion) is published by KAPI. Privacy contact: nico.caetano94@gmail.com (answered within 30 days).

2. What the game does NOT collect

No name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, files, audio, health or biometric data. The game requires no account or sign-up. We operate no game servers and hold no named player database.

3. Processing details

3.1 Game progress (local)

Your progress (levels, cards, virtual currencies, settings), nickname and portrait are stored only on your device (legal basis: performance of contract). They are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data. We have no access to them. You can export your progress as a save code (Settings); the game never sends that code anywhere.

3.2 Optional cloud saves

If you sign in, your save file is stored in your own Google Play Games (Android) or Apple Game Center (iOS) space, tied to your Google/Apple account. We cannot read or list these saves. You can delete them from your Google Play Games data settings or your Apple account settings. See Google's and Apple's privacy policies.

3.3 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows occasional interstitial ads and always-optional rewarded videos. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process your resettable advertising identifier (Android AAID; on iOS the IDFA only with your App Tracking Transparency permission), device information (model, OS, language), ad interaction data and coarse IP-based location (country/city) — for ad serving, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention and, only with your consent, ad personalization. In the EEA/UK, an IAB TCF-compliant consent form is shown at first launch; refusing personalization results in contextual ads only. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your system settings, and the "Remove ads" purchase disables all forced ads (rewarded bonuses are then granted without watching ads). Google acts as an independent controller — see How Google uses data.

3.4 In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing / Apple StoreKit. We never receive any payment data — only an anonymous purchase confirmation used to deliver the item (legal basis: performance of contract). The game contains paid random items (card boosters); drop rates per rarity are displayed in-game before purchase.

3.5 Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

In the EEA/UK, only if you accept audience measurement in the first-launch consent form (purposes "store and/or access information on a device" and "measure content performance" or "understand audiences"; elsewhere, from first launch), the game sends usage events — runs started and ended (mode, wave reached, hero), level-ups, virtual currency spending, screens viewed, ads watched — plus technical data (device model and OS, game version, language, approximate country from the IP address). They are tied to a random installation ID created by Firebase (pseudonymous data), never to your advertising ID, and never used to target ads. Purpose: understanding how the game is played to improve balance, difficulty, usability and retention. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time in Settings → "Privacy choices" (reopens the consent form) or by turning off "Usage stats and crash reports". Recipient: Google Ireland Ltd as our processor (Firebase privacy); we only see aggregated results. Retention: 14 months at most.

3.6 Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the game crashes or hits an error, a technical report is sent: error trace, device model and OS, game version, memory and storage state, and a random Crashlytics installation ID. Purpose: finding and fixing crashes (legitimate interest). Same switch: Settings → "Usage stats and crash reports". Processor: Google Ireland Ltd. Retention: 90 days.

3.7 Notifications

Game reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device: no server or push token is used. Permission is requested after your first run and can be revoked at any time in your device settings.

3.8 Time check

To protect the Game's timers (research, daily rewards) against device clock changes, the Game reads the official time with a technical request to a Google server (www.google.com) at launch and when you return to the app. No game data or identifier is sent: only your IP address is, as with any connection, visible to Google.

3.9 What we do not use

No social network SDK and no analytics tool other than Firebase (no Facebook, AppsFlyer, etc.), and no cross-app tracking beyond the advertising described above. Statistics are never sold or shared for advertising.

4. Recipients & international transfers

Only Google (AdMob, Play Games, Play Billing, Firebase) and Apple (Game Center, StoreKit) receive data, for the purposes above. Their transfers outside the EU (notably to the US) are covered by the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. Retention

Local progress: until uninstall. Cloud saves: until you delete them from your Google/Apple account. Usage statistics: 14 months at most. Crash reports: 90 days. Advertising data: per Google's retention policy. Contact emails: up to 3 years after the last exchange.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. Ads comply with Google's ad content policies.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. In practice: Settings → "Privacy choices" to change your ad and statistics consent; reset/delete your advertising ID in system settings; turn off "Usage stats and crash reports" in the game settings; uninstall the game to erase local progress and destroy the installation ID; delete cloud saves from your platform account; email nico.caetano94@gmail.com for anything else. You may lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (in France: CNIL).

8. Changes

This policy is updated at this address with its version date, before any new online feature is switched on; substantial changes will be announced in-game.
